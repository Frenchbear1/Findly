Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Findly is made by David LaBarre. This policy covers the Findly apps for iPhone, iPad and Windows. The short version: Findly doesn't collect your personal information, and your documents stay on your devices.

What we collect

Nothing. Findly has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your documents, your searches or anything about how you use the app.

Your documents

The documents you add, the search index Findly builds from them, your folders, boards, notes and settings are all stored on your device. Text is read from documents on your device, including Live Text on scanned pages.

Features that use the internet

Some optional features connect to other services, only when you use them:

  • Linked documents. When you link a document to a web address, Findly visits that address to check for newer versions and to download them. Those websites see an ordinary request from your device.
  • Download helper. If you set one up, requests for documents go through a Cloudflare Worker in your own Cloudflare account.
  • Sending to another device. To connect two of your devices, Findly uses a public connection service (PeerJS) that only introduces them to each other. Your library then goes directly between the two devices over an encrypted connection.
  • iCloud. If you turn on iCloud syncing, your library is stored in your own iCloud Drive and handled under Apple's privacy policy.
  • AI requests. If you use the AI helpers, Findly copies a request for you and opens the AI service you choose. Anything you paste there is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

Children

Findly doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
